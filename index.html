00:24FAQ

Questions

Is Quicksnap free?

Yes. No account, no trial, no watermark on exports.

Are my recordings uploaded anywhere?

No. Recording, editing, captions and export all run in your browser, and files stay in your browser's storage.

Which browsers does it work in?

Chrome and Edge get every feature. Firefox and Safari can record, edit and export, without floating controls or instant replay.

Can I record system audio?

Yes on Windows and ChromeOS. On macOS the browser can only capture a tab's audio, so share a tab to include its sound.
